# Lead Journeys

The Lead Journey Report maps out the most frequent and high-converting customer paths, helping you understand how your marketing channels interact and contribute to sales.

---

#### Generate a Lead Journey report

In **Hyros**: **Reporting** → **Create Report** → **Lead Journey** → configure:

1. Attribution Model: pick the model that fits your analysis (e.g. First Click)

2. Key Metric: select Sales to focus on paths leading to purchases

3. Date range: the period to analyze

4. Advanced Options:

- Exclude recurring sales to focus only on first-time purchases

---

#### Reading the report

The report lists the most successful paths to a sale, ranked by performance. For each path, you'll see:

- The sequence of touchpoints (e.g. Meta → Meta → Email)
- How many sales that journey produced
- Total revenue generated by that journey

**Example output:**

| Rank | Journey Path | What it tells you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | Meta Ads → Sale | One touchpoint on Meta is converting leads directly |
| **2** | Meta Ads → Meta Ads → Sale | Two Meta touchpoints lead to higher-intent buyers |
| **3** | Meta Ads → Email → Sale | Meta starts the journey; Email closes the sale |

In this example, Meta Ads is the dominant first touchpoint across the top 3 journeys — but Email is critical for closing in journey #3. Both channels deserve scaling.

---

#### Drilling into specific sources

You can deep-dive into each source in a successful path to see exactly which campaign, ad group, and ad drove those sales:

1. **Source level** → which platform (e.g. Meta)

2. **Campaign level** → which campaign within Meta

3. **Ad Group level** → which ad set within the campaign

4. **Ad level** → which specific ad within the ad set

This tells you precisely which ad to scale for more sales — not just _"Meta is working"_ but _"this specific ad in this specific campaign is what's driving the top journey."_

---

#### Using the data to optimize

The actionable workflow is to identify your strongest journeys, then invest more in the sources that participate in them:

1. Identify the top 3-5 journey paths by revenue or sales count

2. Note which sources appear most often across those journeys

3. Increase budget for those sources (especially the ones that play multiple roles — first touch and closing touch)

4. Drill down to ad level in your strongest journeys to find the exact creatives driving sales

5. Cut investment in sources that don't appear in any top journeys — they may be costing without contributing
