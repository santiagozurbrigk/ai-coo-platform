# Sven

Sven is your AI setup assistant that walks you through connecting your entire marketing stack to Hyros, step by step, app by app, so your tracking is set up accurately from the start.

[Install Sven Extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/hyros-installation-guide/gnpjddbjhhfdcajgdailopgglkagahba?authuser=3&hl=en)

Key points💡

**For 100% tracking accuracy, connect every app your business uses to Hyros.**

Sven scans your business and helps you set up tracking across four key areas:

- 📢 Paid and organic traffic
- 🖥️ Landing pages
- 📅 Webinars, survey forms, and schedulers
- 💳 Checkouts and payment processors

**💡 Don't see your app in the scan list?** Just ask Sven directly, for example: _"Install Facebook Ads"_ , _"Install Stripe" or "install klaviyo"_.

Sven Extension vs. in-app chat assistant — they're not the same.

- **Sven Extension** → your most powerful setup AI assistant. Use it to integrate your apps with Hyros step by step.
- **In-app chat** → for basic questions and support inside Hyros.

When setting up your tracking, **always use the Sven Extension**, not the in-app chat.

#### A. Install the Sven Extension

1. Click Install Sven Extension at the top of this page → Add to Chrome → Add Extension

2. Click the puzzle icon in your browser's top-right → pin Sven

#### B. Scan your Website with Sven

1. Open Sven → enter your website URL → select the ad platforms you use → Continue.

#### C. Review Detected Apps

Sven will show every app it found. Remove ones you don't use by clicking the `×`. Missing apps can be added at the end (see Final Step).

💡 Make sure every app type is covered

Ad platforms, funnel builders / page editors, booking schedulers, survey forms, checkout systems, payment processors, organic traffic sources, and email autoresponders.

#### D. Start the setup

Click **Start** to begin. Sven will walk you through each app, one at a time.

Stay in the same browser tab.

Access all apps **only in the tab where Sven is open**. Switching tabs during setup can break the process.

💡 Want to see your progress?

Click the **three lines** at the top of the extension to see where you are in the setup, or jump to a different platform.

#### E. Finish the setup

Keep going until **every app on your list** is integrated. If Sven missed a platform, ask it at the end (e.g. _"Install Facebook Ads"_ or _"Install Stripe"_) and it'll guide you through that one too.

### FAQ

#### Sven isn't working properly?

If you try to open a new app in a different tab, Sven will automatically redirect you back to the tab where Sven was originally opened. Always access all apps from the same tab where Sven was initially launched. Do not open multiple tabs.

#### Missing an app from your scan results?

If the an app is missing from the scan, when you completed to integrate all apps from your list, you can simply go to the chat and type the following:

- Install Facebook Ads
- Install organic traffic
- Install email

#### You don't see you marketing stack/ apps?

Apps belonging to your business can be viewed in the Menu icon.

#### Can't see your progress?

If you're not sure which apps have already been set up, you need to click the Menu icon. Any app that has been completed will be marked with a green checkmark.
