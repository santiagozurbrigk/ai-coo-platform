# Connected Accounts

This guide shows you how to manage account access in two ways: creating and accessing other Hyros accounts, and controlling which users can access your own account.

---

#### Access Connected Account

In **Hyros**: **profile icon** (bottom-left) → **Settings** → **General** section → **Connected Accounts**. You'll see two options: **Accounts you have access to** and **Users with access to your account**.

Under **Accounts you have access to**:

1. Click Add Account

2. Enter the account's email — or, if you're an agency, paste your agency code

3. Select the appropriate plan based on the tracking revenue limit

4. Click Add Account

---

#### Manage who can access your account

Under **Users with access to your account**, you'll see everyone who currently has access.

**For existing users** — click the **three dots** under Actions to:

- Revoke access
- Hide sensitive data

**To invite a new user:**

- Click Share Access
- Enter their email
- Click Share Account
