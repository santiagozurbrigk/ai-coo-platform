# Total Sales Report

The Total Sales Report provides a summary of all sales made by your customers within a selected date range. It enables you to track and compare total revenue and sales volume across various time periods, customer segments, sources, or tags.

With the built-in Compare Mode, you can analyze two distinct data sets side-by-side, such as sales from different months or specific customer groups — making it easier to identify trends and optimize your strategy.

---

#### Generate a Total Sales report

In **Hyros**: **Reporting** → **Other Reports** → **Total Sales** → configure:

1. Date range (Group A — the first period to analyze)

2. Event type: Sales (or Calls, or both)

3. Enable Compare Mode

4. Second date range (Group B — the comparison period)

5. Tag filter (optional): if comparing by tags instead of dates, filter Group A and Group B by product, source, or other tags

6. Advanced Options:

- Ignore recurring sales if you only want first-purchase sales
- Show only first-time customers if you want to exclude repeat buyers

---

#### Reading the report

Once generated, you'll see:

- A graph showing sales for each group side by side
- Total revenue and sale count for each group, ready to compare

**Example:**

| Period | Total Sales |
| --- | --- |
| **February** | 3,983 |
| **March** | 7,062 |

In this example, March clearly outperformed February — almost doubling the sale count.

---

#### Using the data to make decisions

The actionable workflow is to find what changed between the two groups and act on it:

1. Identify which month, period, or segment performed better

2. Analyze what strategies, campaigns, or offers were live in the better-performing period (e.g. March in the example above)

3. Replicate or scale those strategies in future periods
