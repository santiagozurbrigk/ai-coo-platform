# Cohort Analysis

The Cohort Report is a performance reporting tool that groups leads or customers by when they first entered your system allowing you to compare long-term metrics like revenue, sales, and LTV across different time periods.

#### How to Use Cohort Report

---

#### More in Depth

---

#### Generate a Cohort Analysis report

In **Hyros**: **Reporting** → **Other Reports** → **Cohort Report** → configure:

- Time frame: the period covering the cohorts you want to compare (e.g. September–November)
- Source filter (optional): filter by traffic source or tag via Specify Attributes
- Advanced Options: apply any additional filters needed for your analysis

Click **Generate Report**.

---

#### Reading the report

The report shows each cohort's revenue and sales month by month after acquisition.

**Switch views** to analyze cohorts from different angles:

| View | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Revenue by month** | How much each cohort earned over time |
| **Total Sales Overview** | Cumulative revenue from each cohort to date |
| **ROI** | Return on ad spend over time for each cohort |

---

**Spotting healthy vs declining trends — Example**

**Healthy cohort pattern:**

> ROI: Month 1 = **108%** → Month 3 = **125%** → Month 6 = **150%**

The cohort is _gaining value over time_ — marketing nurturing or upsells are working. Scale the tactics that acquired this cohort.

**Declining cohort pattern:**

> ROI: Month 1 = **108%** → Month 3 = **52%** → Month 6 = **54%**

The cohort starts strong but flattens — initial sales were profitable, but no recurring revenue or upsells materialized. The business may need new offers, retention strategies, or different audience targeting.

---

#### Using insights to scale

1. Compare cohorts side by side at the same time horizon (e.g. "all cohorts at month 3 after acquisition")

2. Identify what made the strongest cohorts strong — ads, offers, timing, audience

3. Replicate winning conditions in future months

4. Watch for declining cohorts — if newer cohorts underperform older ones at the same time horizon, your acquisition quality may be degrading

5. Forecast accurately — use historical cohort patterns to predict how current cohorts will mature
