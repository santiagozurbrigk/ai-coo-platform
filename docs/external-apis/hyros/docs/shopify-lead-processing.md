# Enable/Disable Lead Processing

Learn how to enable or disable lead processing for your Shopify integration to prevent inflated lead counts.

## When to Disable Lead Processing

In rare situations, you may have an inflated number of leads that were not leads prospecting your business. This type of leads you don't recognize may come from instances when you update a contact in Shopify or perhaps after sending an email from Shopify.

## How to Disable Lead Processing

In cases where you see inflated leads, you can block them from processing by going to your Shopify integration inside your Hyros account

[here](https://app.hyros.com/external-services/cart-integration/shopify)

and turn

**OFF**

the

**Process Lead Events**

toggle:
