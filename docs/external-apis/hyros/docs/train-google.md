Send Hyros attribution data to Google Ads for optimized campaign performance.

## Overview

Choose the best option for sending Hyros events to Google Ads based on your current setup.

There are two main approaches to sending Hyros events to Google Ads. Choose the option that best fits your current setup:

## Setup Options

[Option B – Sending Hyros Events to a Brand New Google Pixel / New Conversion Action](./option-b-sending-hyros-events-to-a-brand-new-google-pixel-new-conversion-action.md) — Start fresh with a new conversion action powered by HYROS
