# Date of Click Attribution

The Date of Click Attribution feature presents the full user journey according to the date of the click, instead of the date of the sale or event.

---

#### How Date of Click works

Every Hyros sale has two relevant dates:

- **Date of Sale** — when the customer actually purchased
- **Date of Click** — when they originally clicked the ad that started the journey

By default, reports use **Date of Sale**. The Date of Click setting flips this — backdating each sale to the day the original click happened.

---

#### How to read the impact

Example

Imagine your report shows **11 sales** on **May 24**. With Date of Click turned ON:

- **1 sale** stays on May 24 — the click and sale happened close together
- **10 sales disappear** — the original clicks were from January, April, and other earlier dates that fall outside your selected date range

The 10 sales weren't lost — they're now attributed to the dates their clicks actually happened. If you expand the date range to include those earlier click dates, they reappear.

| Setting | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Date of Click OFF** (default) | 11 sales on May 24 — all sales that _closed_ that day |
| **Date of Click ON** | 1 sale on May 24 — only sales whose _click originated_ that day |

---

#### Why this matters

If you ran a Facebook ad in January and the customer bought in May, the default Date of Sale view credits the May 24 column.

That makes it hard to see whether your January ad spend actually paid off — the revenue shows up in a month you didn't run that campaign.

**With Date of Click ON:**

- The sale is attributed back to January
- You can compare ad spend and revenue within the same time period
- Long-cycle ads stop appearing unprofitable just because the revenue takes time

This is essential for businesses with **call funnels, demos, high-ticket sales, or any long click-to-sale time**.
