# Paperform Tracking

Tracking customer emails entered on paperform can be achieved in just 2 steps:

1. First step is to copy the universal script which you can find [right here](https://app.hyros.com/settings/tracking/universal-script).

2. When editing your form in Paperform, go to “Configure” then “Analytics”. Paste the universal script in the field labelled “Page load-scripts” as shown here:

Repeat this with any form you would like to track. We would then suggest running a test by completing the form just like a lead would. Your email should be inside Hyros in the “CRM” tab, which you can find [HERE](https://app.hyros.com/#/mh/sales-data/leads) in your Hyros account.

If you do not see this, please reach out to the support team with a link to your form and the email you used to test and we will be happy to investigate further for you.
