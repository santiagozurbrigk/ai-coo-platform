# LTV Forecasting

## How LTV Forecasting Grows Revenues (VIDEO DEMO)

---

#### What does this help me with?

LTV Forecasting will allow you to drill down by source and then using all of your historical data, will give you a forecast into the potential LTV within a 1, 3 and 6 month period per source based on either brand new customers only or all customers, including returning ones.

Use this if you’d like to answer questions such as:

- _“How much can I expect to actually make from this source in the longer term?”_
- _“How much should my cost per customer be to make a profit within a 1/3/6 month period?”_
- _“How much should my cost per sale be to make a profit within a 1/3/6 month period?”_

---

#### How forecasting works

When you connect Hyros, it ingests your financial history (Stripe, Shopify, etc.) and builds a **predictive model** based on how your past customers behaved.

When a new customer comes in, Hyros compares their behavior to similar past customers and predicts:

- How much additional revenue they'll generate over weeks, months, or years
- For SaaS: MRR, churn rate, refund rate over time
- For ecommerce: repeat purchase patterns and total customer value

Example #1 — How forecasting works in Ecommerce

A customer comes to your site and buys a **$10 t-shirt**. Hyros looks at all your past customers who behaved similarly and finds that, on average, those customers come back and spend an additional **$200-300 over the next 6 months**.

**Without forecasting:**

> Ad cost: $20 → Revenue: $10 → Looks like a loss → Pause the ad

**With forecasting:**

> Ad cost: $20 → Initial revenue: $10 → Forecast 6-month revenue: $300 → **Real ROI: 15x** → Scale the ad

The ad that looked unprofitable upfront is actually one of your best investments.

Example #2 — How forecasting works in SaaS / subscriptions

For SaaS, Hyros forecasts:

| Metric | What it predicts |
| --- | --- |
| **Long-Term Value** | Total revenue per customer over your chosen timeline |
| **MRR Trajectory** | How monthly recurring revenue from a cohort will grow |
| **Churn Rate** | Expected cancellation rate over time |
| **Refund Rate** | Expected refund frequency |

**Worked example from a real SaaS ad report:**

| Time horizon | Ad spend | Revenue back |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **First 30 days** | $1,400 | $1,800 |
| **6 months** | $1,400 | ~$2,800 |
| **12 months (forecast)** | $1,400 | $2,800 (doubling investment) |

The first 30 days look like a marginal win, but the **12-month forecast** shows the ad doubles your investment. With this data, you can scale aggressively, knowing the ROI is locked in over time.

---

#### Forecasting by Traffic Source

As Hyros gathers more data, forecasts get more granular — you can see different long-term values **by source**:

- YouTube traffic might forecast 3x more revenue per customer than Facebook
- TikTok customers might churn faster than Google customers
- Email leads might convert at higher trial-to-paid rates than paid social

This lets you make budget decisions based on **which sources produce the most valuable long-term customers** — not which sources deliver the cheapest upfront clicks.

## FAQs

#### Which Reports are Forecasting Metrics Available?

- Last click
- Scientific
- First click

#### How are these Sales Attributed?

The sales and forecasted LTV are attributed to the source based on the attribution mode selected.

For example if you load a report in last click mode, then we will calculate the LTV forecast based on the products attributed to the source in last click mode.

This differs from our standard LTV reports where LTV is always shown based on first click.

#### Can I Import Previous Data?

You can import sales for the past year from Stripe and Shopify if you’ve just joined Hyros in order to assist with Forecasting and LTV reports.

Please take in mind however that these reports will not be attributed to a source, so when looking at sources you will not be able to see the past data as attributed.
