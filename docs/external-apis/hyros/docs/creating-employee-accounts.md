# Sub-Users/ Creating Employee Accounts

This guide will show you how to give your employees or sub-users limited access to your account.

If the person you are providing access to is an agency and/or they already have a Hyros account, you can instead provide them access to your account directly from theirs. Just follow the guide [**HERE**](https://docs.hyros.com/docs/managing-client-accounts).

Otherwise, please see the video below to add sub-users to your account:

Please note:

If you are trying to add a sub-user account and receive the error message "Cannot use this email. Please use a different email address," it means that this email is already being used sub-account under another Hyros account.

You can use the +1 option to modify the email—for example:

john.doe+1@hyros.com

.

---

#### A. Open the Sub Users Menu

In **Hyros**: **profile icon** (bottom-left) → **Settings** → **Sub Users** → click the **Add Sub User** icon.

#### B. Enter the User's Details

Fill in:

- First name
- Last name
- Login email
- Password

Click **Next**.

#### C. Set their Access Level

By default, a new sub user has **full access**. Adjust it before finishing:

**Restrict features** — toggle off any areas you don't want them to access (e.g. webhooks).

**Restrict data visibility** — control what data appears in their reports:

- Limit to a specific source, category, or goals
- Limit to a specific ad account
- Choose whether they can view data with or without attribution

Once configured, click **Add Sub User**.
