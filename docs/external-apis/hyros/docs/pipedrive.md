# Pipedrive

This document explains the steps required to link your Pipedrive account to Hyros for tracking sales and leads.

#### A. Connect Pipedrive

1. In Hyros: profile icon → Settings → Integrations → search Pipedrive → [→ Connect Pipedrive](https://app.hyros.com/external-services/cart-integration/pipedrive)

2. Name the integration (anything you like) → log in with your Pipedrive account → follow the prompts to authorize

## Lead Stages

Inside your Pipedrive integration, you can create Lead Stage Rules which will map specific events inside your Pipedrive account and use them to create lead stages inside Hyros:

You will be able to map contact labels inside of Pipedrive to lead stages in Hyros.
