# Attribution Gap

View the Attribution Gap of your Hyros account right from your Dashboard to see the percentage of attributed entities versus unattributed.

## Overview

If what you want is to see the Attribution Gap of your Hyros account right from your Hyros Dashboard, then this widget is exactly what you're looking for!

Just go to your Hyros Dashboard and click on the

+ Add Widget

box, then click on

Top Table

and select

Attribution Gap

.

This widget will show you the percentage of attributed entities and the percentage that is not attributed.

You will be able to choose the entities you want to use to measure your attribution gap from a list showing Leads, Sales, and Carts.

By default, the Leads option should be selected, but you can change it.
