# Unique Sales Widget

Get a quick look at your Unique Sales from one or several traffic sources.

## Create the Widget

This widget allows us to get a quick look at our Unique Sales from one or several traffic sources. To configure it please click on the

Add Widget

button in your Hyros Dashboard, click the

Report

section and select

Unique Sales

:

## Configure Widget Type

Then select the widget type to use depending on your preferences and needs:

Total value

shows the whole group of sales and the

Attributed value

takes into account the attribution mode (Last click/Scientific). Both methods have optional filters for sources, categories, add/exclude leads, etc.

After the options have been configured please click on

Insert

to add the widget to your dashboard.
