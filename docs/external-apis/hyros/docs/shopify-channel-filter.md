# Filter Shopify Sales by Specific Channel

Learn how to filter your Shopify sales events by specific channels for more accurate tracking.

## Why Filter Sales by Channel?

There are certain scenarios in which we may want to filter our events depending on the channel they were generated from. Because of this, Hyros allows us to filter the channel(s) to be taken into account when configuring your Shopify Integration.

## Select Channels to Track

Go to **Settings → Integrations** and click on **Configure** below **Shopify**, then click on the Edit icon. Scroll down to **Sales Channel Filters** to activate this feature. If you want to stop receiving sales from a specific channel, you can filter them by deselecting the channel from the list. All channels, including newly created ones are enabled by default.
