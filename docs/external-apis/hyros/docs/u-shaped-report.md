# U-Shaped Report

This guide explains how to use U-Shaped attribution in Hyros to understand how different touchpoints contribute to conversions.

---

#### Generate a U-Shaped Report

In **Hyros**: **Reporting** → **Performance Report** → set **Attribution Model** to **U-Shaped** → choose your **time frame** → **Generate Report**.

---

#### How it works — Example

Imagine a customer's journey looks like this:

1. Click on a Bing ad (first click)

2. Click on a Google ad (middle click)

3. Click on a Meta ad (last click)

4. Make a $200 purchase

Here's how revenue is distributed:

| Source | Position | Attribution % | Revenue Share |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Bing** | First click | 40% | $80 |
| **Google** | Middle click | 20% | $40 |
| **Meta** | Last click | 40% | $80 |
