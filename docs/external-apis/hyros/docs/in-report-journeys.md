# POWER FEATURE : In-Report Journeys

Customer journeys, Cohorts and GEO-location are now part of every standard report in Hyros. We've reengineered all of these key advanced reports into a single, more powerful report which improves the way you read the data and take decisions.

## How In-Report Journeys Works (VIDEO DEMO)

Note that Cohort Analysis

is accessible ONLY in First Click mode.

---

#### Where to find in-report tools

When viewing any report (e.g. a Last Click report), you'll see **spyglass icons** alongside your data. Each icon opens a deeper view of the row you select:

| Icon | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| 🔍 **Customer Journey** | The exact click path leads took before converting |
| 📍 **Geographic Breakdown** | Where each segment of customers is located |
| 📦 **Product Breakdown** | Sales, refunds, costs, and profit per product |
| ⏳ **Cohort Analysis** | How spending evolves over time for customers in this segment |

---

#### 🔍 Customer journeys

Click the journey icon on any row to see the **exact sequence of touchpoints** that segment took before converting.

Example use:

- Click **Facebook** → see that Facebook customers typically go Facebook → Google → Pinterest before buying
- Click **Email** → see that email customers often follow a completely different path

This reveals which sources work together — and helps you understand the real role each channel plays, not just where the click came from.

---

#### 📍Geographic breakdown

Click the location icon on any row to see **where the customers in that segment are located**.

Use this to:

- Identify your highest-revenue regions for a specific source or product
- Refine geographic ad targeting for the segments that matter most
- Spot regional patterns you'd miss in a standard report

---

#### 📦 Product breakdown (essential for ecommerce)

Click the product icon to see a full breakdown of the products driving sales in your segment:

- Sales per product
- Cost of goods
- Refund rates
- Net profit

Some products may be losing you money even when ads look profitable.

Top-line ad performance can hide product-level losses — an ad that generates revenue might be selling products with terrible profit margins or high refund rates. Always check product breakdown before scaling an ad, especially if you sell multiple SKUs.

---

#### ⏳Cohort analysis

Click the cohort icon (hourglass) on a row in a First Click report to instantly see cohort data for those leads:

- Average spending per cohort over time
- Whether week-1 customers are spending more or less than week-5 customers
- Whether your spending rate per customer is improving or declining as new cohorts come in

This is the same cohort analysis as the standalone Cohort Report — but pre-filtered to the segment you were looking at, saving you from configuring it manually.
