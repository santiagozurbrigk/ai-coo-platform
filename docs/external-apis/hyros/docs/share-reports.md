## 2. Export Dashboards

Another great way to share quick reports with your team is to export them PDF file. To do this we just need to click on the

Print View

button on the upper right hand side of our dashboard for the PDF file to be generated and downloaded on your device:

The resulting file will show the same data your dashboard had at the time the document was generated including date range and all active widgets.
