# How To Fix No Sources

When you are experiencing a lot of “No Source” sales on the reporting board, it can be frustrating. Especially if tracking is set up right. Use this guide to fix the problem.

---

#### How to Diagnose

Before fixing anything, find out where the untracked customers actually came from:

1. In the No Source area, click the graph icon under the Sales column to pull up the buyer history

2. Click a customer's email → open their Click History

3. Scroll to their first interaction with your business — this reveals the likely source

Whatever you find there tells you which of the four issues you're dealing with.

---

#### Issue 1 - Ads Missing the Tracking Parameter

**What you'll see:** Customers coming from a platform (e.g. Instagram) with **no Hyros tracking parameter** in the URL.

**What it means:** They clicked your ad, but the ad's URL is missing the Hyros parameter, so the click wasn't tracked.

Fix

Add the tracking parameter to **all** your ads. See Issue 1 in the written guide below (includes two articles on checking your ad tracking).

---

#### Issue 2 - Ad Account Not Connected Correctly

**What you'll see:** Customers coming from Facebook or Instagram **with** the tracking parameter present, but still **no source identified**.

**What it means:** Your ad account isn't integrated correctly — if it were, a source would be generated from that parameter.

Fix

Reintegrate your Facebook Ads or Google Ads. See Issue 2 in the written guide.

---

#### Issue 3 - Untracked Organic Sources

**What you'll see:** Customers arriving from organic channels — YouTube, Instagram organic posts, social media — with no tracking.

**What it means:** These organic sources don't have tracking set up, so their traffic comes in unattributed.

Fix

Identify where organic visitors come from and add tracking to those sources. See the organic tracking article below.

---

#### Issue 4 - Untracked Funnels or Pages

**What you'll see:** Customers who are **completely untracked** — a purchase or call with little or no click history at all.

**What it means:** They came through a funnel or page that's **missing the Hyros tracking script** in its header, or the script is installed incorrectly.

Fix

Look at where these sales/calls are entering and identify which funnel lacks tracking. Check that the Universal Script is correctly installed in the header of every page in that funnel. See the email tracking article below if they're coming from emails.

---

#### Still seeing No Source entries?

If you've worked through all four issues and still see No Source sales, leads, or calls, reach out to the support team.
