# Geo Sales Data

The Geolocation Sales Report shows where your leads and sales are coming from, helping you identify the most profitable regions, countries, or cities to target with your ads.

---

#### Generate a Geo Sales report

In **Hyros**: **Reporting** → **Other Reports** → **Geo Sales** → configure:

1. Attribution Model: pick the model that fits your analysis (e.g. First Click)

2. Date range: the period to analyze

3. Traffic source: filter via Specify Attributes (e.g. Facebook for Meta Ads)

4. Advanced Options:

- Show new customers only to focus on fresh acquisitions
- Ignore recurring sales so the data reflects first-time purchases
- Exclude specific products if certain products would skew the analysis

---

#### Reading the report

The report organizes your data across two views:

1. Geographic breakdown:

- Country level — broad market overview
- Region level — state, province, or other regional breakdown
- City level — granular targeting opportunity

You'll see leads, sales, and revenue for each location — drill down from country to city as needed.

2. Device breakdown:

Click the **Devices** tab to see how sales split across desktop, mobile, and tablet.

**Example output:**

| Country | Leads | Sales | Revenue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **United States** | (highest) | (highest) | (highest) |

In this example, US customers brought in the most sales and revenue — a clear signal to scale Meta Ads targeting in that country.

---

#### Using the data to scale

The actionable workflow is to identify your strongest geographies and devices, then double down on them:

1. Identify your top-performing countries (then regions, then cities)

2. Allocate more budget to those locations in your ad campaigns

3. Adjust audience targeting to match the device most of your customers use — if most buyers are on mobile, prioritize mobile-optimized creative and bidding

4. Test new locations that look promising but aren't yet at scale
