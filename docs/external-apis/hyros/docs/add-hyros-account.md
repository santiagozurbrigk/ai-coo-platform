# Add A HYROS Account For Yourself Or A Client

This guide will show you how to add a new account for yourself or a client. If you are an approved agency you can also receive a commission by entering your First Promoter code.

Please note that you may apply to get referrals for your agency by going

[here](https://hyros.com/affiliate-grow)

. Please use the first promoter guide in the video and text below to generate commissions for your referrals.

1

## How to add a HYROS Account to your Agency

---

#### What this does

Walks you through adding and billing your first client account in the Hyros Agency program — choosing commission or discount, selecting their plan, and getting them set up with a checkout link.

---

#### A. Add the Client Account

Click **Add Account** → enter the **login email** for whoever will pay for the account _(typically your client's email)_.

#### B. Choose Commission OR Discount

Decide how you want to structure the account:

- Commission (default) — you earn a commission. Make sure your FirstPromoter code is entered (see the FirstPromoter setup video)
- Discount — apply a 20% discount for your client, which automatically applies across all pricing tiers

#### C. Select the Pricing Plan

Choose the tier matching your client's monthly revenue _(e.g. "up to $40,000/month")_. If you need a refresher on pricing, the pricing sheet is in the Agency Hub.

Select the tier → confirm the billing period (e.g. monthly) → click **Add Account**.

#### D. Send the Checkout Link

The new client account now appears in your account list. To bill them:

Click the **three dots** under the **Actions** column → **Copy Checkout Link** → send it to whoever's paying.

Once they pay, they'll show as **Active** in your Hyros account.

## How To Receive Commissions On Added Client

---

#### What this does

Links your FirstPromoter referral code to your Hyros agency account so that clients you add generate commissions in your FirstPromoter account.

---

#### A. Get your referral code from FirstPromoter

In your **FirstPromoter dashboard**: scroll down to your **referral link** → copy **only the text after the**`=`**sign**.

#### B. Add the code in Hyros

In **Hyros** (your agency account): **profile icon** (left menu) → **Settings** → **Referral Program** → **Join Program** → **I'm Already a Promoter** → paste your referral code → **Update Promoter Code**.
