# Sales Mapping using Shopify

Learn how to configure which order status Hyros should count sale for your Shopify store.

We understand every business is unique and because of that we want you to have full control regarding the particular step of the process in which we are going to count your sales.

To take advantage of this feature, please go to your Shopify Integration from

**Hyros → Settings → Integrations**

and click on configure below

**Shopify**

, then click on the

**Edit**

icon:

Then scroll down to the

**Events to be processed**

section and select the event status Hyros should consider a Sale from now on (options available are Order Created, Order Paid, Order Fulfilled). After that, please click on the

**Update State**

button to confirm the changes.
