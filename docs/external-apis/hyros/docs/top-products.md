# Top Products Widget

View overall stats for a specific product or product category right from your Hyros Dashboard.

## Overview

If you want to see the overall stats for a specific product or a product category right from your Hyros Dashboard then the Top Products widget is exactly what you are looking for!

Just go to your Hyros Dashboard and click on the

+ Add Widget

box, then click on

Top Table

and select

Top Products

:

## Configure Widget Preferences

Once here we can configure our Widget preferences like include/exclude tags, ignore recurring sales, exclude hard costs, filter by product or product category, filter leads by tag, from

Specify Attributes

and select the metrics we want to see from

Select metrics

. After the options have been configured please click on

Insert

to add the widget to your dashboard:
