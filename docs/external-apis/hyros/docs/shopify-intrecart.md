# Intrecart Integration for Shopify

Configure Intrecart for SMS cart recovery tracking with HYROS on your Shopify store.

If you are using Intrecart with Shopify, complete the

[Shopify set up](https://marketplace.gohighlevel.com/docs/shopify)

in the step above and then alert your direct onboarding rep. We will complete the Intrecart set up for you.

Contact your onboarding representative or reach out to our support team to have Intrecart configured for your account.
