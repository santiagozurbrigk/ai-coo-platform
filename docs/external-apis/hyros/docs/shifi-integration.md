# ShiFi

This doc will show you how to track your ShiFi sales using Hyros

1

## Integrate ShiFi

#### A. Connect HYROS to ShiFi

Start inside your HYROS account.

In Hyros: **profile icon** (bottom-left) → **Settings** → **Integrations** → **ShiFi**

#### B. Log in to your ShiFi account

In the ShiFi login page, enter your **email** and **password** and log in.

#### C. Verify the connection in ShiFi

1. Inside your ShiFi account, navigate to Settings > Integrations.

2. Click the HYROS tab within the Integrations section.

3. If the integration is active, you'll see a green confirmation message. This means ShiFi is connected and ready.

Once the integration is active the platforms interact in two ways:

**Tracking Script:** ShiFi automatically pulls your Hyros tracking script and injects it onto

your ShiFi checkout pages. This ensures customer clicks and leads are properly mapped

to your ad data.

**Order Mapping**: When a financing contract is officially funded, ShiFi pushes an Order

into Hyros. This order includes the customer's email and the funded amount, closing the

loop so the revenue attributes directly back to the original lead.
