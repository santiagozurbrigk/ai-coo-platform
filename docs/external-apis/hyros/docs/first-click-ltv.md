# First Click LTV

The First Click LTV model tracks how much revenue a lead generates over time, starting from their very first interaction with your business. This helps you see which traffic sources are truly profitable in the long run.

## Overview

This report allows you to answer key questions like:

- Which traffic sources are worth investing in for long-term profitability?
- Is my cost per lead lower than the revenue (LTV) generated from that lead?
- Which campaigns are driving profitable long-term customer value?
- What's the revenue attribution of purchases over a specific period after the first click?
- How much revenue do leads generate over time based on their first interaction with a traffic source?

---

---

#### Generate a First Click LTV report

In **Hyros**: **Reporting** → **Create Report** → set **Attribution Model** to **First Click LTV** → choose:

- Date range of the report (the acquisition period)
- LTV date range (how far forward to measure value)
- Specify Attributes → filter by traffic source (e.g. Google)
- Click Generate Report.

---

#### How it works — Example

You want to know the 3-month LTV of leads who first clicked a **Google ad in February**:

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Date range of report** | February (full month) |
| **LTV date range** | 3 months |
| **Traffic source filter** | Google |

The report will show:

- Every lead whose first click was a Google ad in February
- All revenue those leads generated within 3 months of that first click

If the cost per lead for a campaign is lower than the LTV

The campaign is profitable; consider scaling. If higher → the campaign is losing money over time.

A campaign showing negative ROAS and negative profit

This means **ad spend exceeded total revenue** within the LTV window.
