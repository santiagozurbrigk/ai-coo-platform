\# PROJECT CONSTITUTION

Project Codename:  
AI COO

Version:  
MVP V1

\====================================================  
MISSION  
\====================================================

Build the operational operating system for infoproduct businesses.

The goal is not to build another CRM.

The goal is not to build another dashboard.

The goal is not to build another project management tool.

The goal is to build an AI-powered operational brain that understands how a company works and helps founders improve operations, sales performance, team management and documentation.

The software should become:

The single source of truth for the entire organization.

\====================================================  
THE BIG VISION  
\====================================================

Long-term vision:

When a founder enters the platform, they should immediately understand:

\- what is happening  
\- why it is happening  
\- where bottlenecks exist  
\- where money is leaking  
\- which team is overloaded  
\- which process is broken  
\- what should be prioritized next

without needing to manually investigate.

The software should behave like:

an AI COO.

Not a chatbot.

Not a dashboard.

An executive intelligence system.
